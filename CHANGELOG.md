## [0.2.0]

### Added 
1. Interactive BVH rendering with `MeshCat` and `Viser` viewers.
2. FABRIK inverse kinematics for chains and BVH clips.
3. Motion analysis for foot contacts and floor estimation, center of mass, support polygons, stability, and planar root-motion decomposition.
4. Space-time retargeting with various constraints support.
5. `skeleton_tree` for ASCII representation of skeleton hierarchy.

### Changed

1. Refactored the package around `Joint`, `Hierarchy`, and `Motion`, with new module paths and APIs.
2. Updated BVH I/O, forward kinematics, motion operations, IK, and viewers for the new data model.
3. Reworked retargeting with explicit joint mapping, chain refinement, and shared space-time optimization.
